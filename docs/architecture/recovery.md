# Recover from observed evidence

Project record replacement uses an exclusive durable intent, previous/proposed hashes and a same-directory temporary record. A target at the previous hash plus verified temp permits replacement. A target already at the proposed hash finishes bookkeeping. Conflicts, missing proposals or unsupported records retain all evidence and stop.

Version creation reserves a number before copying, verifies a complete stage, then promotes and transfers the working marker. Partial copies preserve their expected source identity. Never allocate a new number to hide an interrupted operation.

Delivery reserves UUID/tag before capture. Incomplete copies remain staging evidence. A coherent saved capture is authoritative on ordinary retry. For an explicit user-selected pre-package replacement, save the complete choice/inventory before copying; retain earlier snapshot/evidence, resume the same promotion, and refresh portability and final completion evidence. Any package file, saved remote identity, draft attempt or occupied tag prevents replacement.

Package once. A saved ZIP and descriptor govern all later retries. Journal draft attempts before dispatch; complete empty listings after uncertainty do not prove non-dispatch. Reconcile the same UUID/tag/descriptor until the owned draft appears or independently retained non-dispatch proof permits another attempt. Verify exact uploaded assets and publication before closing final orders. A lost closure response retries closure and bookkeeping only.

Observed cancellation applies before capture and before publication. Published deliveries stand. Local progress, versions, packages and reservations remain. Assignment changes compare activation UUIDs so completing an earlier delivery preserves a later assignment.

Inbox saves immutable reports before disclosure and receipts afterward. Missing receipts replay saved information. Downloads use a verified temporary destination and preserve earlier verified receptions and conflicting artifacts. Unknown archive/record formats, hashes or paths stop without destructive cleanup.
